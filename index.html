                          Bluetooth earbuds

                          Temperward talks through whatever your phone is using for sound. Pair your earbuds in Android Settings → Bluetooth and the coach's voice goes to them automatically. Take them out (or switch to Speaker mode) to talk out loud.

                          Devices

                          ⌚ Watch & buzz

                          One short buzz = new step. Three = done. Long buzzes = danger. If your phone has no buzz motor, nothing happens.

                          👓 Small screens

                          Watch view: big step and a big Next button, for a small screen or a phone on your wrist. HUD view: high contrast, huge text, for glasses-style displays.

                            Settings

                            Look & language

                            Spanish changes the buttons and main screens. Built-in guides are in English for now; custom AI guides follow your language.

                            Voice

                            AI (optional)

                            Paste a free Gemini API key to turn any custom goal into real steps, make your day plan smarter, translate, and turn on live camera coaching. The key is saved only on this device and sent only to Google's Gemini API. Without a key, everything still works with the built-in guides.

                            Life Mode

                            Camera Coach

                            Privacy

                            Everything stays on this phone. Your plan, money, health notes, contacts, Rise progress, and jobs are saved only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you add a Gemini key: then only the text of a custom goal or plan request, or a small camera snapshot while live coaching is on, is sent to Google Gemini. Camera frames are never saved. Stop camera turns the camera fully off.
